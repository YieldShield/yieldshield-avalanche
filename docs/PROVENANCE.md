# Provenance

The Avalanche pilot is newly authored in this repository, informed by YieldShield's existing Monad/Base/BNB split-risk product design. It is a reduced, independent implementation, not a claim that the underlying split-risk idea is new. No private keys, deployment registries, environment files or historical contracts are copied. The MIT license retains YieldShield's copyright. React, Vite, viem and OpenZeppelin remain subject to their upstream licenses. Demo assets are explicitly unrelated to issuer-backed USDC.
